# Canon check

Work in three passes, and don't start the second until the first is complete.

1. Extract. List every claim the piece makes or implies about the world: names, titles, relationships, ages and dates, places, events, rules of how the world works, and what an image or scene shows. Implied claims count: "her brother's old sword" claims a brother. `references/claim-types.md` lists the kinds that get missed.
2. Check. For each claim, find the canon heading that settles it with `read_canon`. Give it one status: locked, absent, or contradicted. Quote the single canon line that decides it.
3. Decide. Contradicted or absent claims block. Proposed additions are listed separately and still block. Only an all-locked piece clears.

A claim you can't resolve after reading the relevant sections is absent. Uncertainty blocks.
