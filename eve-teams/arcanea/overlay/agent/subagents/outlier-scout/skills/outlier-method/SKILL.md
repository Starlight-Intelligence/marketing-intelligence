# Outlier method

1. Baseline. Prefer the creator's own median over their last 20 comparable pieces. When that isn't visible, use the median of a named peer set of at least five similar creators in the same format and window. Write down which baseline you used.
2. Multiple. A piece is an outlier at three times its baseline or more on the metric that matters for the format: saves or shares for posts, watch time or completion where shown for video, comments that ask a question for community pieces. Views alone qualify nothing.
3. Exclude. AI-generated work, work you can't attribute to a person, paid placements, and pieces older than the window.
4. Why. Name the mechanism: the first line or first two seconds, the structure, the specificity, the timing against an event, or the community it reached. One mechanism per record, the one you'd bet on.
5. Transfer. What Arcanea could borrow, and what it couldn't because it depends on that creator's audience or identity.

Write each record in the format in `references/record-format.md`.
