# Weekly read

1. Rank on would-pay signals first, then saves and shares, both per thousand reach. `references/signal-ranking.md` lists which events count as which.
2. Signups and views appear in the table as context columns. They never decide the order, including as a tie-breaker.
3. Two pieces are tied when their ranking signal differs by less than the noise band in the reference. Say "tie" rather than inventing an order.
4. The read has three parts: the ranked table, three calls (make more, stop, try next week), and the caveats with sample sizes.
