# Signal ranking

## Would-pay signals (rank first)

- paid conversion attributed to the piece
- preorder or deposit
- visit to the pricing or checkout page from the piece
- a reply, comment, or DM asking how to buy, what it costs, or when it ships
- waitlist joins that chose a paid tier or entered payment details

## Save and share signals (rank second)

- saves and bookmarks
- shares, sends, and reposts with added commentary
- quote posts that recommend the piece

## Context only (never rank)

- views, impressions, reach
- raw signups, follows, and free waitlist joins
- likes

## Normalizing

Divide each ranking signal by reach in thousands. When reach is missing for a piece, rank it on raw counts in a separate list and say why.

## Noise band

Below 1,000 reach, treat differences under 50 percent as a tie. From 1,000 to 10,000, under 20 percent. Above 10,000, under 10 percent. These are working rules for small samples, not statistics; say so when a call rests on one.
