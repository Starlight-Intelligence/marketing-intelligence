# Visual gate

Score ten criteria from 0 to 3 each, for a total out of 30. The criteria and what each score means are in `references/gate-criteria.md`.

- 26 to 30 ships.
- 22 to 25 iterates. Name the two or three fixes that would move the score most, in order.
- 0 to 21 restarts from the brief. Say what the brief got wrong, since a weak render usually comes from one.

Two conditions block a ship regardless of the total: no `.vis.provenance.json` sidecar, and a canon contradiction. Report both in the band line.

Give one line of evidence per criterion, pointing at something in the frame. A score with no evidence doesn't count.
