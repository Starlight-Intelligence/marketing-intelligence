# Arcanea style

Arcanea writes like a person who has been inside the world and is telling you one true thing about it. Specific beats grand. A scene beats a claim about scenes.

Reject on any of these:

- a contrast formula that knocks down a claim nobody made
- a one-line closer that restates the point as a slogan
- stock AI vocabulary; `lint_against_style` checks `references/banned-words.json`, and `references/patterns.md` covers the cousins it can't
- an em dash anywhere
- bold used for emphasis

Every piece also needs one Arcanea-only detail: a fact from the canon, a scene a reader could picture, or a number only Arcanea has. A detail any fantasy brand could write doesn't count. `references/patterns.md` has examples of the difference.
