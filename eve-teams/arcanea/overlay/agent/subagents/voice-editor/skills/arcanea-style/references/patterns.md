# Patterns

## Contrast formulas

The writer sets up a claim nobody made so the real point can knock it down.

- "It's not just a game. It's a world."
- "This isn't about X. It's about Y."
- "Less X, more Y."

Fix: state the real point directly and give it the evidence the contrast was standing in for.

## One-line closers

A final line, often its own paragraph, that restates the piece as a slogan.

- "And that changes everything."
- "The future is here."
- "Welcome to the new era."

Fix: end on the most specific line in the piece, or end one sentence earlier.

## Vocabulary cousins

The lint catches the listed words. Their cousins carry the same smell: "transformative", "next-level", "immersive journey", "rich lore", "boundless", "breathtaking", "dive into", "harness", "supercharge", "reimagine".

## Rhythm tells

- three adjectives in a row
- a rhetorical question answered in the next sentence
- an opener that announces the topic ("Let's talk about...")
- every paragraph the same length

## Arcanea-only detail

Doesn't count, since any fantasy brand could write it: "a vast world full of ancient secrets."

Counts: a named place or character from the canon doing something specific, a scene with one concrete sensory detail, or a real number from the product or community. Confirm lore with `read_canon`.
