# Outlier comparison

The records in `references/outliers/` describe human work that beat its peers by a wide margin, and why. Read them with `glob` and `read_file`.

1. Pick the one or two records closest to the draft in platform and format.
2. For each, compare the opening, the structure, and the specific detail. Name, in one line each, what the draft does worse.
3. Don't tell the writer to copy the outlier. Name the mechanism that worked and let them find Arcanea's version.

When the folder holds only its README, there are no records yet. Say so and skip the comparison rather than comparing against memory.
