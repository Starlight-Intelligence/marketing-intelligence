## Evidence it outperformed
Guinness World Records lists this video as the most viewed Dark Souls lore video on YouTube, 1,871,703 views as of 2 March 2017, and a separate record for his Dark Souls 3 main bosses lore video at 1,558,772 views as of 30 November 2016 (https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/453690-most-viewed-dark-souls-3-lore-video-on-youtube). The comparison is other Dark Souls lore videos on YouTube. Know Your Meme (https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/people/vaatividya) is reported via search to cite his Bloodborne explanation at over 5.8 million views. Current count: not verified.

## The move that transfers
He turns fragmentary item descriptions and environment details into one continuous narrated story with a clear character at the center, so a scattered lore base becomes something a newcomer can follow in one sitting. A recognizable character plus a cause-and-effect arc is the structure, not the specific game.

## What not to copy
The authority comes from tightly sourced in-game text of a game the audience already plays. His delivery and "prepare to cry" framing are personal brand and would read as imitation.
