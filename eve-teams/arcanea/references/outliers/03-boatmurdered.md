# Boatmurdered
- Creator: A group of 14 Something Awful forum members, thread started by user TouretteDog
- URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boatmurdered (archive: https://lparchive.org/Dwarf-Fortress-Boatmurdered/Update%201-30)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: Serialized forum Let's Play of Dwarf Fortress written as in-world narrative, November 2006 to March 2007. Opened via search result only; WebFetch failed on every host this run.
- Category: lore-drop
- Move: relay-authorship-with-inherited-ruins

## Evidence it outperformed
Wikipedia, as summarized in search, credits the story with showcasing the game's emergent storytelling and bringing the game and the Let's Play format to a wider audience. No view or sales numbers were seen. The Let's Play Archive still hosts the full run. Comparison is qualitative: it is the best-known example of its form. The 2b2t wiki page notes a Minecraft base named after it, evidence of lasting cultural reach (https://2b2t.miraheze.org/wiki/Boatmurdered). No hard numbers: not verified.

## The move that transfers
Each player ran one in-game year, then handed the fortress on, so every new author inherited the ruins and grim engravings left by the last. Lore accumulated as artifacts and consequences rather than exposition, and the relay structure made readers want the next handoff.

## What not to copy
It depends on a simulation that generates unpredictable disasters. A single-author fantasy cannot borrow that randomness, and the 2006 forum culture is gone.
