## The move that transfers
It presents an invented place as a discovery inside an existing world, in the form fans already use to find real secrets. Because the format signals "found, not made," the lore spreads as a question (is this real?) before it is read as fiction.

## What not to copy
Passing off mod content as genuine datamined material depends on an existing game's trust and risks being read as deception. Use a disclosed, in-fiction frame in your own setting.
