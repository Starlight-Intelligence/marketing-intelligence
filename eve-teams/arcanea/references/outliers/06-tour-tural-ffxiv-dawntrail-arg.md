# Tour Tural travel-agency ARG (Final Fantasy XIV: Dawntrail)
- Creator: Square Enix with agency We Are Royale (studio work, credited as a team)
- URL: https://weareroyale.com/case-studies/final-fantasy-xiv-dawntrail-arg/
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: 17 day ARG hidden in a fictional travel-agency website, 2024. Opened via search result only; WebFetch failed on every host this run.
- Category: lore-drop
- Move: mundane-frame-with-hidden-lore

## Evidence it outperformed
Royale's case study describes fans noticing odd travel advisories, hidden notecards and torn map pieces released about six a day. A Royale headline reports a GEMA microsite gold award (not verified). No traffic or engagement numbers were found in the sources seen: not verified. Evidence is the agency's own account, not independent press.

## The move that transfers
The world is introduced as a mundane in-world service (a travel site) whose copy slowly turns strange, so reveal and discovery are the same act. Releasing map pieces on a drip schedule gives the community a daily task and a shared completion goal.

## What not to copy
It needs a large existing player base and an agency budget. A small creator can use the frame but not the scale; do not cite it as independent proof of results.
