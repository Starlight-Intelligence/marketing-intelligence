## The move that transfers
The PV sells a cast and a mood in one short piece, letting the protagonist's design and the studio's visual voice carry the announcement rather than plot explanation. A long-awaited adaptation is paired with a clear, singular tonal promise, so the reveal reads as a statement of intent. Reuse the structure: one signature visual register, a few character beats, no exposition.

## What not to copy
The reach came largely from pre-existing manga fandom and MAPPA's reputation; a new IP will not get that baseline. Do not copy the specific cinematic grading or the character shots.
