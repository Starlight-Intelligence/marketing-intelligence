## Evidence it outperformed
TechRaptor reports pre-registrations at 16,337,022 at time of writing, with 20 million and 30 million named as next milestones (same URL). Compared against genre norm: the same search surfaced Zenless Zone Zero at over 40 million pre-registrations by mid-2024 (https://videogames.si.com/news/zenless-zone-zero-surpasses-pre-registration-goal, via search result only), so Wuthering Waves is a strong but not leading figure; I did not isolate the effect of the character trailer. Trailer views: not verified.

## The move that transfers
The roster is shown in a character trailer while a visible counter and reward milestones drive sign-ups, so each new character reveal doubles as a prompt to join. The audience sees faces, a number moving, and a next goal at the same time. Any creator with a waitlist can pair character drops with public milestones.

## What not to copy
Large pre-registration counts in mobile gacha are inflated by reward incentives and the Genshin-adjacent audience. The reward structure does not fit an indie waitlist.
