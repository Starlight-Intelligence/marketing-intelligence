## The move that transfers
The trailer lets a character known for menace simply walk through familiar stages and dispatch beloved characters, so the reveal is the clash of two established worlds rather than a spec sheet. Staging the audience's rumor mill first and then answering with something larger creates the reaction content. Build reveals around a single memorable action and the right music cue.

## What not to copy
It relies on decades of shared cultural memory and a crossover; a new character cannot borrow that. The music and the crossover premise are specific to this IP.
