## The move that transfers
The trailer introduces a new protagonist, her relationship to the first game's lead, and a handful of recurring gods in a familiar painted style, so existing fans instantly read the continuity. A single deliberate mystery figure gives the community a puzzle to argue over. Reveal the new face and leave one question open.

## What not to copy
It is a sequel to a hit with an award-winning predecessor; the surprise and goodwill are earned. Do not copy the mythology or the specific mystery.
