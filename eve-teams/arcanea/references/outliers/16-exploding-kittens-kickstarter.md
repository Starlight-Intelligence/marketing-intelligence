# Exploding Kittens (Kickstarter)
- Creator: Elan Lee, Shane Small and Matthew Inman (The Oatmeal)
- URL: https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/elanlee/exploding-kittens (page fetch failed in this run with a DNS error; opened via search result only)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: Kickstarter campaign page plus pitch video, January 2015
- Category: trailer-launch-copy
- Move: comic-voice-pitch-with-fast-first-day

## Evidence it outperformed
- 219,382 backers, the most of any Kickstarter project at the time, per Kickstarter's announcement quoted by https://www.geekwire.com/2015/exploding-kittens-blows-up-kickstarter-as-most-backed-project-of-all-time/ (search result only). Previous record holder Reading Rainbow had about 105,857.
- About $8.78 million raised, the most-funded Kickstarter game, per https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/exploding-kittens-the-most-backed-kickstarter-project-ever (search result only). Not the top funded overall: the Coolest Cooler exceeded $13 million.
- Backer figures differ by a digit between outlets (GameSpot printed 219,832); I used the Kickstarter-quoted figure. I did not see the pitch video or page copy this run, so I cannot verify claims about its wording.

## The move that transfers
A low-priced, instantly understood product paired with a well-known comic voice lets the pitch sound like the brand rather than like a pitch. A low price point drives backer count more than dollar total, and backer count is the number that gets press. The transferable lesson is to match the pitch's tone to the product's tone and to price for volume when virality is the goal.

## What not to copy
It leaned on The Oatmeal's large existing readership and art style. The absurd-humor register will not suit a serious product.
