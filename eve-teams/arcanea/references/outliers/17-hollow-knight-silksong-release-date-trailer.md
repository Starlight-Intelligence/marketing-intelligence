# Hollow Knight: Silksong release date reveal trailer (Gamescom 2025)
- Creator: Team Cherry
- URL: not located directly; the trailer itself was not opened. Coverage: https://www.notebookcheck.net/Hollow-Knight-achieves-new-all-time-peak-player-count-on-Steam-following-Silksong-release-date-reveal.1094768.0.html (search result only)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: short trailer on YouTube and Steam, August 2025
- Category: trailer-launch-copy
- Move: restraint-and-date-as-the-payoff

## Evidence it outperformed
- Over 3 million YouTube views in under a day, later almost 4 million with 458,000 likes, per Notebookcheck and Insider Gaming (https://insider-gaming.com/hollow-knight-peak-player-record-silksong-release-date/, search results only). One Newegg forum post gave 4.6 million; undated, treat as not verified.
- Silksong reported at about 4.8 million Steam wishlists, the most wishlisted game on Steam (https://www.newegg.com/gamer/community/t/news-the-most-wishlisted-game-on-steam-finally-has-a-release-date-after-all-these-years/3278, search result only). Alinea Analytics figure of nearly 5 million is secondhand.
- The original Hollow Knight hit a new all-time Steam concurrent peak (23,840 on 22 August 2025 versus 20,300 in May 2022) after the reveal. The comparison here is against the game's own earlier peak, not peers. I did not find a studio-wide trailer comparison.

## The move that transfers
After years of silence, the payoff was a short piece that delivered the one thing the audience asked for, a date, without hype copy. The announcement also lifted the previous game, so a launch message can pull in a back catalog. Reusable idea: when demand is already built, the most persuasive launch piece is the plainest one that answers the open question.

## What not to copy
This depended on years of fan anticipation and the studio's refusal to market. A new IP cannot rely on the same silence.
