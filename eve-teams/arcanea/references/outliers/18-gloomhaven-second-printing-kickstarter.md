# Gloomhaven (Second Printing) Kickstarter
- Creator: Isaac Childres / Cephalofair Games
- URL: https://www.kicktraq.com/projects/1350948450/1681361753/ (tracker page, search result only; the Kickstarter page itself was not opened)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: Kickstarter campaign, 28 days, 4 April to 2 May 2017
- Category: trailer-launch-copy
- Move: second-launch-from-proven-demand

## Evidence it outperformed
- Raised about $3,999,796 from 40,642 backers (Kicktraq, search result only). Wikipedia gives about $4 million from over 40,000 backers (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gloomhaven, search result only).
- Compared against the creator's own first campaign in September 2015: $386,104 from 4,904 backers, roughly ten times more money.
- Mid-campaign reports from Crowdfund Insider showed about $1.6 million in the first week, passing $2 million with 22,000 backers and nearing $3 million with 30,700 backers (https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=98364, https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/?p=99374, search results only). I did not see the page copy this run.

## The move that transfers
A second launch can use the first campaign's reception, reviews and fulfilled promises as the main argument, which replaces hype with proof. Pacing of updates and stretch goals in the weeks after launch kept the total climbing. The transferable idea: if you have shipped once, make the second pitch about the evidence that you delivered.

## What not to copy
The numbers come from a niche heavy-board-game audience and a product with word-of-mouth momentum. The copy itself is not verified here, so do not model its wording.
