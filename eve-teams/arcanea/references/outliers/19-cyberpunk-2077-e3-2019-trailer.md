# Cyberpunk 2077 E3 2019 cinematic trailer (Keanu Reeves and release date)
- Creator: CD Projekt Red
- URL: not opened. Coverage: https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/cyberpunk-2077-most-viewed-e3-game-trailer/ (search result only)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21
- Format: trailer at Xbox E3 briefing, 9 June 2019, YouTube
- Category: trailer-launch-copy
- Move: stack-two-reveals-in-one-trailer

## Evidence it outperformed
- Most viewed game trailer of E3 2019 on an unofficial tracker (Mike Armbrust's E3 YouTube Interestometer): about 19.66 million views against Marvel's Avengers at 17.34 million and Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order at 8.65 million (VGC, search result only). Another outlet reported lower figures (17 million, 16 million and 11 million) at a different time.
- Caveats: ComicBook.com (https://comicbook.com/gaming/2019/06/13/cyberpunk-2077-e3-trailer-ps4-xbox-pc/, search result only) noted the margin over Avengers was slim, tracking days differed, and some counts may be inflated by ads. Only valid for E3 2019, not an all-time claim.
- I did not verify a comparison with CD Projekt Red's other trailers.

## The move that transfers
The trailer carried two separate hooks at once, a celebrity role and a firm release date, which gave press two headlines from one piece. Pairing a person reveal with a date turns an ordinary trailer into a news event. A new reveal can be staged so it lands live at a show, not before.

## What not to copy
Paid celebrity casting and a major platform stage cannot be copied by small teams. The game's launch problems later also changed how this trailer is read.
