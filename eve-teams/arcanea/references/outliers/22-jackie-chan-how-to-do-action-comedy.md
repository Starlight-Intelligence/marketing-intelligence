Channel context: over 1.3 million subscribers at the end of its first run (https://www.videomaker.com/videonews/2017/12/every-frame-a-painting-officially-comes-to-an-end/).

## The move that transfers
It takes one practitioner and extracts a small set of teachable principles, each proved with clips so the viewer sees the technique rather than hearing about it. The essay is short and the principles are numbered, so it is easy to summarize and share. A craft piece that names a method gives every other creator in that field something to apply.

## What not to copy
The specific Jackie Chan footage and the channel's editing rhythm. Clip-heavy analysis also carries fair-use risk that a new creator must handle on their own.
