Terms like "hard magic" and "soft magic" are now common vocabulary among fantasy writers, which few craft essays achieve.

## The move that transfers
The essay gives craft advice a name and a single sentence rule, then places existing famous works along a spectrum so readers can locate their own project. It frames the rule as a guideline from experience, not a mandate, which invites use and debate. Naming a concept that writers were already circling lets others adopt the term in their own discussions.

## What not to copy
Calling your advice a "law" without comparable standing in the field. The hard and soft magic vocabulary is Sanderson's and should be credited, not rebranded.
