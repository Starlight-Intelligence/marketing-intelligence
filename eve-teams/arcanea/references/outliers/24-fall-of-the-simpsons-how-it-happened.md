## The move that transfers
The video poses a question the audience already argues about (why did a beloved thing get worse) and answers it as an ordered causal story rather than a list of complaints. Starting with a widely held belief gives built-in search demand and comment-section debate. The same channel applies the format to anime and manga, so it moves between fandoms.

## What not to copy
The Simpsons subject and the channel's narration style. A "fall of X" video on a niche fandom lacks the existing mass audience that this one tapped.
