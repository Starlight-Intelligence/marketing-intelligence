## The move that transfers
The essay explains a half-understood idea by tracing its lineage (earlier hypertext writing and prior essays) and then stating its principles plainly, which makes it a default reference people link to when introducing the topic. Being the clearest single explainer of a trend turns a personal essay into infrastructure other writers cite. Visual illustration by the author's own hand supports the idea in a way plain text would not.
