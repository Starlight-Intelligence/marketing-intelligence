# Hollow Knight Kickstarter campaign (Team Cherry)
- Creator: Team Cherry (small Adelaide team, three developers)
- URL: https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Hollow_Knight_Kickstarter (secondary page describing the campaign; the Kickstarter page itself could not be opened). Also https://www.pledgebox.com/post/hollow-knight-kickstarter
- Date opened: 2026-10-21 (opened via search result only; WebFetch failed with a DNS error this run)
- Format: Kickstarter campaign page for an indie game, 19 November to 19 December 2014 per one source
- Category: indie-launch
- Move: small-goal-world-first-pitch

## Evidence it outperformed
Search results (hollowknight.wiki and pledgebox.com, seen via WebSearch only) put the campaign at roughly A$57,000 against a A$35,000 goal. Backer counts conflict across sources (2,158 most cited, 1,158 in one). The starting audience was not verified. Compared against its own goal, it cleared it with the Hornet stretch goal unlocked. Treat all figures as not verified against Kickstarter itself.

## The move that transfers
Set a goal low enough to be near-certain, then sell the world (a hand-drawn insect kingdom) rather than the feature list, so stretch goals extend the lore (a new playable character) instead of padding scope. The campaign made the setting the product.

## What not to copy
The specific art style and Metroidvania genre positioning. The 2014 Kickstarter climate does not match today's.
