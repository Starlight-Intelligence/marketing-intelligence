# Murder Drones pilot episode (Glitch Productions)
- Creator: Liam Vickers (Glitch Productions)

- Format: animated web pilot on Glitch's YouTube channel, premiered 29 October 2021; series later ran eight episodes to August 2024
- Category: indie-launch
- Move: pilot-as-complete-hook

## Evidence it outperformed
Per WebSearch summaries of TV Tropes (https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Trivia/MurderDrones), the pilot became the most-viewed video on Glitch's main channel within about a week and Glitch grew from about 500k to 600k subscribers in that period. A World Screen report (https://worldscreen.com/?p=335306) cites over 270 million series views and a Prime Video licensing deal. These are secondary, undated figures, not verified.

## The move that transfers
The pilot is a self-contained short with a hard tonal contrast (cute visuals, violent premise) that makes the whole premise legible in one sitting, and it ends on an open question that sends viewers to the channel and fan spaces. Release it as a standalone piece, not a trailer.

## What not to copy
The established Glitch channel gave it a distribution base most solo creators lack. The cute-versus-gore tone is its own signature.
