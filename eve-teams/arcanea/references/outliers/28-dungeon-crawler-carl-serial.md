## Evidence it outperformed
Via WebSearch summaries: Wikipedia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Crawler_Carl) and the No Film School article report a Royal Road start in 2019, more than 20,000 Patreon supporters per a Gig Harbor piece (https://www.gigharbornow.org/?p=52451), sales figures from almost 8 million to a headline claim of 10+ million (conflicting, the higher number not verified), and an NBCUniversal TV rights deal reported by https://www.kold.com/2026/05/21/tucson-author-talks-dungeon-crawler-carl-success-tv-deal/. Royal Road follower counts and the starting audience were not found. Compared against typical web serial outcomes it is an outlier, but I have no peer baseline numbers.

## The move that transfers
Publish chapters on a free serial platform on a steady cadence, let readers fund the continuation (Patreon), and convert to books only once demand is proven. The serial doubles as market research and a built-in audience for the paid edition.

## What not to copy
The LitRPG game-system premise and comedic voice. The timing relied on genre readers on Royal Road, which may not fit other genres.
