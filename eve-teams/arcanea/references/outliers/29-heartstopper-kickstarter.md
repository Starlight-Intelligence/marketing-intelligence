## Evidence it outperformed
Per WebSearch summaries: the Bookseller reported the 9,000 GBP goal was hit in two hours, with 23,278 GBP from over 500 backers early on; a later Bookseller item (https://thebookseller.com/rights/hcg-publish-oseman-s-online-graphic-novel-869686) cites almost 60,000 GBP total and more than 60,000 subscribers when Hachette acquired it. Starting audience: Oseman said she planned print at 50,000 online readers. Compared against the campaign's own goal. Not verified directly.

## The move that transfers
She set a public readership threshold before announcing print, so the campaign launched into an audience already asking for the book. A modest goal funded fast gives visible momentum.

## What not to copy
The LGBT YA romance audience on Tumblr in 2018 was a specific, highly engaged community, and the Netflix outcome came later and is not a reproducible result.
