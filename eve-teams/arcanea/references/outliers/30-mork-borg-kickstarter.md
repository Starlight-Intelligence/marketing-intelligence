# Mork Borg Kickstarter campaign (Ockult Ortmastare Games and Stockholm Kartell)
- Creator: Pelle Nilsson and Johan Nohr
- URL: https://www.backerkit.com/projects/jnohr/mork-borg (BackerKit mirror of the campaign data; Kickstarter page not opened)
- Date opened: 2026-10-21 (opened via search result only; WebFetch failed with a DNS error this run)
- Format: Kickstarter tabletop RPG campaign, 17 May to 16 June 2019
- Category: indie-launch
- Move: art-direction-as-brand-identity

## Evidence it outperformed
Per a WebSearch summary of the BackerKit page: roughly 489,000 SEK raised against a 50,000 SEK goal from 1,152 backers (not verified directly). An EN World roundup (https://www.enworld.org/posts/7968331/) names the creators and Free League as publisher. Later spinoffs were reported by ICv2 (https://icv2.com/articles/news/view/50584/pirate-borg-spotted-off-starboard-bow) at about 57,000 USD for Pirate Borg. Starting audience not found.

## The move that transfers
An unmistakable visual identity (a doom-metal, zine-like look) carried the pitch, and the rules were permissively licensed so others could build on the world, which then marketed it for free. The setting's style did the selling.

## What not to copy
The apocalyptic metal aesthetic and the open license model only fit games designed to be hacked. Copying the neon-and-black look would read as imitation.
