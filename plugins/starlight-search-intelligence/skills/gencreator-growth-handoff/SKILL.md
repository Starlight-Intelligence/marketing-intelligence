# GenCreator Growth Studio handoff

Take a search intelligence finding into production without turning Starlight into a creator-facing CMS. Resolve the owning brand, unique message and product promise; reuse brand rules and assets from the existing GenCreator / FrankX content library rather than assuming one voice.

Deliver an evidence-anchored content package: canonical webpage/topic, long-form editorial outline, one demonstration/video concept, one LinkedIn-native proof-led angle, email or newsletter angle, distribution timing hypothesis, ethical partner/affiliate disclosure where relevant, and source-to-asset references. Avoid identical cross-posts and fabricated before/after performance.

Connect the artifact to an owned CMS route or draft system when an authorized connector is present. Capture distribution URLs/UTMs and a meaningful conversion event. Hand off to `gencreator-native` or existing content operations when installed; absence is not permission to publish.

**Acceptance:** one approved source of truth, differentiated channel derivatives, readable creative QA, verified links and a measurement plan. Starlight retains source provenance; GenCreator owns production and publishing.
