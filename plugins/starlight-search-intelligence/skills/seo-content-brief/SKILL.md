# Evidence-backed content brief

Produce **one publishable brief** for one intent cohort. Resolve target reader, differentiation, owned offer, intended conversion and current top-ranking pages before outlining. Reuse current product marketing context.

When Semrush is available, use exact `phrase_organic` SERP report schema; otherwise inspect live public results through authorized sources and mark time and geography. Identify the authentic reader questions, competing formats and uncovered evidence. Never reproduce competitor text.

Deliver: target query/intent; promise; reader job; original thesis; evidence-to-obtain; title/H1 candidates; recommended sections; expert proof/assets; potential FAQ based on real questions; internal links; original diagram/video opportunity; schema eligibility check; CTA; measurable baseline and publication QA. Cite external factual claims.

Do not write fabricated testimonials or return a keyword-stuffed draft. Pair GEO readiness with accessible HTML and precise sourced statements; do not assert a particular structure guarantees LLM citations. Hand off to GenCreator Growth Studio if the brief requires a multi-channel editorial package.
